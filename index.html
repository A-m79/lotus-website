Setup

One command. Fully protected.

Every Lotus command lives under a single, organized /lotus — no hunting through ten different slash commands to find the one you need.

› /lotus setup
1
Add Lotus with Administrator

Required so it can actually act on threats — quarantine, revert, lock down. Every Lotus command still checks its own, stricter permission on top — see the full command list.

2
Run /lotus setup

Creates the LOTUS SECURITY category with your logs, alerts and quarantine channels, plus a join-gate role and verification channel. Already have your own join gate? Run /lotus setup verification:false to skip that part.

3
Tune it to your server

Set your default punishment with /lotus config punishment, exempt trusted members or roles with /lotus whitelist add, and check everything at a glance with /lotus config status — or just leave the defaults.

Good to know

Before you add it, the honest answers.

No marketing fluff — just what Lotus actually needs and does with your server's data.

Why does Lotus need Administrator permission?

Reverting a malicious role edit, quarantining a compromised account, or locking down channels during a raid all require elevated Discord permissions — a bot without Administrator simply can't act fast enough to stop a nuke in progress. You can review and audit everything Lotus does through /lotus case and the activity logs channel.

Is the Free plan actually free, or a trial?

Free is free forever — no feature timer, no watered-down detection window. Anti-Nuke, Anti-Raid, Anti-Spam, the verification gate and the full manual moderation suite are all included at no cost. Premium adds extra detection layers (alt accounts, phishing links, permission watch) and forensic tools on top, it doesn't gate the core protection.

What data does Lotus store about my server?

Server config (thresholds, channels, punishments), security case logs, and — only if you turn it on — activity logs. Lotus does not read or store the content of every message; content is only scanned in-memory for spam/phishing patterns. Full detail in the Privacy Policy.

Can I remove Lotus at any time?

Yes, at any time, with no lock-in. Removing Lotus from your server stops all monitoring immediately, and its stored data for your server is deleted within a reasonable delay afterward.
